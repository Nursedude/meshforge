# Q-CLAIM — persistent-queue claim races (2026-10-23)

Moved verbatim from the ROADMAP row on 2026-11-18 (MF012 room; the row keeps the title + this pointer).

Found by the BIC-2 slice-2 non-author re-review, 10-23; PRE-DATE BIC-2:

- (a) a slow worker's late `mark_failed` lands on a row `cleanup_stale` already reset to pending (and maybe re-claimed), bumping retry_count twice for one attempt;
- (b) a late `mark_delivered` flips a dead-lettered row to delivered after its DROPPED was counted (leg shows both; `_fire_terminal` fires twice);
- (c) `mark_failed` is read-modify-write with no compare on the retry_count it read.

Cure class: a per-claim token (or compare-and-swap on status+retry_count) so only the current claimant can resolve a row — design first (grill), then test-first. Added by a session (reason: correctness of the queue the BIC-2 legs read from).
