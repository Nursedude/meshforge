## Rules this keeps

- Subscribe, never bridge: a mosquitto bridge to public loops and duplicates (the moc3 own-id confusion).
- Uplinking is a community decision: fleet radios carry HawaiiNet, no public LongFast — an uplink publishes other people's traffic and positions.
- Topic ≠ geography: `msh/US/HI/#` sees only gateways that chose that root.
- MQTT is a Meshtastic-only lens; the protocol-neutral RF view is the SDRs + CLAW witnesses + MeshAnchor.
