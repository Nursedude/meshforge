## (a) Interoperating with HawaiiMesh / regional public Meshtastic mesh without co-dependence

### Takeaway
MeshForge already sits on the regional public mesh as a **participant, not a dependent**: fleet radios keep the public LongFast primary untouched and add a private `meshforge` channel and the community `HawaiiNet` channel (default key), and all inter-box traffic rides the operator's own RNS/LXMF and localhost MQTT — never the regional broker. The only recorded plan to touch HawaiiMesh infrastructure (HI-OBS) is to *feed* the community's existing CoreScope instance as an observer, not to build a rival.

## (b) Default PSK (AQ==) / LongFast channel utilization and congestion

### Takeaway
MeshForge has a purpose-built, firmware-source-grounded **Channel Load** pane (25 % / 40 % knees) and a 2026-10-03 measurement that the busiest fleet box logged 155 "Ch. util >25%. Skip send" lines in 3 h. It treats `AQ==` as publicly known (warns against it on private brokers) but uses it as the default for the public/nodeless profile; congestion thresholds for user-facing advice are explicitly NOT yet measured.

## (d) EmComm claims and honest-capability framing

### Takeaway
MeshForge's EmComm posture is defined by its honesty doctrine: "the truth told when we cannot see", unobservable ≠ healthy, "Up is presence, not function", and an explicit public-sentence discipline that downgrades even its strongest bridge claim to a single dated lab run.
